## Chapter One

The lamp had not turned in nine years, and still the town set its clocks by it.

Maren walked the shingle at low tide, counting the things the sea gives back. A boot. A
bottle. On the ninth morning, a name she thought she had buried.

## Chapter Two

*Later,* she would say it began with the letter. It did not. It began with the light.
